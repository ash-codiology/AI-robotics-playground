# Module 5: Foundations of Embodied Intelligence

This module covers the theoretical and practical foundations of embodied intelligence - the idea that intelligence emerges from the dynamic coupling between an agent's cognitive processes, its physical form (morphology), and its sensorimotor interactions with the world.

## Contents

- `index.md`: Main theoretical content covering principles, history, applications, and challenges of embodied intelligence
- `examples-and-exercises.md`: Practical examples, programming exercises, and hands-on projects

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, students should be able to:

1. Explain the core principles of embodied intelligence and how they differ from traditional AI approaches
2. Analyze how physical embodiment contributes to intelligent behavior
3. Implement simple embodied systems that demonstrate emergent behaviors
4. Evaluate the advantages and limitations of embodied intelligence approaches
5. Design experiments that test embodied intelligence hypotheses
